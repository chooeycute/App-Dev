# The Heirs

![The Heirs Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/3/3c/The_Heirs_Poster.jpg)

**The Heirs** is a South Korean drama series starring *Lee Min-ho* and *Park Shin-hye*.  
It tells the story of rich high school students dealing with love, friendship, and family expectations.

---

## 🌟 Main Characters
- **Kim Tan** – The heir to the Jeguk Group.
- **Cha Eun-sang** – A hardworking girl from a poor family.
- **Choi Young-do** – Kim Tan’s former best friend and rival.

---

## 💭 Why I Love This Series
> “The Heirs” shows that love and dreams can exist no matter your status in life.  
> I really enjoyed the emotional moments and the chemistry between the main leads.
